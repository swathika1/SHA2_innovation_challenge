AI and Predictive Modelling Disclosure

The Platform uses a computational reinjury risk model to generate risk scores based on patient biomechanical data, medical history, and rehabilitation progress.

Important: Reinjury risk scores are computational aids only. They do not constitute a medical diagnosis, prognosis, or clinical recommendation. Clinicians remain solely responsible for all treatment and rehabilitation decisions. Patients must not act on any risk score without consulting their assigned clinician.

The AI model may be updated periodically. Past outputs do not guarantee future accuracy.

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how SHA2 Rehab Coach collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal data. It applies to all users of the Platform including patients, clinicians, and caregivers.

This Platform operates in Singapore. Your personal data is handled in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA) of Singapore.

What Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Account credentials: Email address, hashed password, role.
  • Personal details: Full name, date of birth, phone number, postal code.
  • Medical history: Diagnosed conditions, past surgeries, current medications, allergies, family medical history — entered directly by you or uploaded via document scan.
  • Biomechanical assessment data: Pose and movement data captured during rehabilitation sessions.
  • Injury records: Historical and current injury information linked to your profile.
  • Usage logs: Session timestamps, feature interactions, and system-generated activity records.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used for the following purposes:

  • Calculating reinjury risk scores using the Platform's predictive model.
  • Displaying your medical and rehabilitation history to your assigned clinician.
  • Enabling clinicians to create, monitor, and adjust personalised rehabilitation plans.
  • Generating session summaries and progress reports visible to you and your clinician.
  • Sending appointment reminders and clinical notifications.
Model retraining: Your data is not currently used to retrain or update the predictive model. If this changes, you will be notified and your consent will be sought separately before your data is used for that purpose.

Who Can See Your Data

Access to your data is strictly role-based:

  • Patients can view only their own records, history, and risk scores.
  • Clinicians can view records belonging only to patients assigned to them. No cross-patient visibility is permitted.
  • Caregivers can view basic progress information for the patient they are linked to.
  • Platform administrators have system-level access for maintenance and support purposes only, subject to internal access controls.

No user can access another user's data outside of the permissions described above.

Data Storage and Security

  • Data is stored in a SQLite database on a secured server within the NUS infrastructure.
  • Passwords are stored as one-way cryptographic hashes — they are never stored in plain text.
  • Data in transit is protected using HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Access to the server and database is restricted to authorised personnel only.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Upon account deactivation:

  • Non-clinical data (account preferences, usage logs) is deleted within 30 days.
  • Clinical records that have been reviewed or verified by a clinician are retained for a minimum of 5 years in line with Singapore healthcare records guidelines.
  • You may request deletion of your personal data by contacting us (see below). Requests involving clinician-verified records will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis, and retention may be required by law or for medical continuity.

Your Rights Under the PDPA

As a data subject under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct any inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Withdraw consent for the collection, use, or disclosure of your personal data, subject to legal and contractual restrictions.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at: sha2rehab@nus.edu.sg

We will respond to all data access and correction requests within 30 days.

Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third-party organisations for commercial purposes. Your data is not disclosed to external parties except:

  • Where required by Singapore law or a valid court order.
  • Where necessary to prevent serious harm or protect patient safety, in line with PDPA exceptions.

Account Termination and Data

If you leave the rehabilitation programme or your account is deactivated, the data retention policy described above applies. You will not be able to access the Platform after deactivation, but you may request a copy of your personal data before deactivation is finalised.

For any questions about these documents, contact sha2rehab@nus.edu.sg
